# Phase 05 — AIOps & Autonomous Operations

> **Status: 🔜 Coming soon** — content and capstone project are in progress.

## What this phase will cover

Phase 05 is where infrastructure starts to **act on its own** — not just alert you, but detect problems, decide what to do, and execute runbooks with guardrails.

**Topics planned:**

- **AIOps fundamentals** — anomaly detection on metrics and logs, correlating signals, reducing alert noise
- **Self-healing patterns** — Kubernetes operators, automated remediation (restart, scale, rollback)
- **Predictive operations** — forecasting load, proactive scaling before saturation
- **Autonomous incident lifecycle** — detect → diagnose → act → verify → document (with human approval gates)
- **Feedback loops** — measuring whether automated actions actually helped

**Planned capstone (working title):** A self-healing service demo — an operator or controller that detects a failing deployment, rolls back or scales, and posts an audit trail to Slack.
